Request Account Deletion

Senior Concierge is operated by Alberts Opportunity LLC. We respect your privacy and provide a way to request deletion of your account and associated personal data.

How to Delete Your Account

You can request deletion directly in the app:

  1. Open the Senior Concierge app.
  2. Sign in to your account.
  3. Open the Account screen.
  4. Select Delete Account and follow the instructions.

If you cannot access your account, you can request deletion by emailing our support team.

Request Account Deletion

What Data Is Deleted?

An account deletion request covers your Senior Concierge account and associated personal information, including your profile and service booking records, subject to applicable legal retention requirements.

Data Retention

Certain records may need to be retained when required by law, including records necessary for tax, legal, or regulatory obligations. Such information will be retained only as required or permitted by applicable law.
