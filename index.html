About Senior Concierge Coast

Senior Concierge Coast is operated by Alberts Opportunity LLC. Our mission is to help older adults enjoy greater independence, connection and peace of mind through thoughtful everyday assistance.

Our services are non-medical. We do not provide emergency response, nursing care or other licensed medical services.

Everyday Concierge Services for Older Adults

Whether you need help for yourself or a loved one, Senior Concierge Coast offers practical, personalized non-medical support. Contact us to discuss your needs and confirm availability.

Errands & Grocery Shopping

Help with grocery shopping, picking up everyday items and handling routine errands.

Transportation Coordination

Assistance arranging transportation to appointments, community activities and local destinations.

Companionship

Friendly conversation, shared activities and social connection during everyday visits.

Appointment Scheduling

Help arranging appointments, organizing schedules and keeping track of everyday plans.

Light Household Assistance

Practical support with simple household tasks and non-medical daily routines.

Meal Preparation

Assistance preparing simple meals and organizing everyday meal routines.

Technology Assistance

Patient guidance with smartphones, tablets and everyday technology.
